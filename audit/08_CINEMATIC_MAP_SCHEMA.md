## 0. WHY THIS FILE EXISTS

Session 8's structural validation proved only that the map had **111 rows × 41 columns, no empty cells, no duplicate ids, well-formed ids**. That is *shape* validation. It is not *contract* validation.

Semantic inspection found that **typed fields carried non-typed values**:

- `duration_ms` — a declared `INTEGER` field — held `E-DEFAULT` (15 rows) and `E-HERO` (1 row).
- `spring_or_easing` — a declared 4-value enum — held `1400`.
- `overlap_ms`, `local_focus_scale`, `shared_element_id` held free prose.
- `subject_exposure` held undifferentiated vectors of unexplained arity.

⛔ **No free-form value may appear in a typed numeric or enum field.** That single rule is the origin of this document.

**Provenance of the corruption (evidence).** The map was machine-generated by `audit/runtime/session-08/_tooling/normalise-map.ps1` from two pipe-delimited sources (`08_map.psv`, `08_map_cj.psv`). #### 2.2.1 `subject_exposure` positional grammar (explicit)

A comma in a typed exposure field is **not** a free-form list. It is a **fixed-arity positional vector**, bound by position to the row's own attention columns:

| Position | Binds to | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `primary_attention` | exposure of the PRIMARY subject |
| 2 | `secondary_context` | exposure of the SECONDARY subject |
| 3 | `secondary_context` (second named element) | exposure of a TERTIARY equal-weight subject; legal only where the beat names three |

`context_exposure` is a **different** field: it records the *context band* exposure, not the secondary subject's exposure. This is why `I11` legitimately carries `subject_exposure = 1.00,1.00` together with `context_exposure = 0.55` — the Finance figure and the Homi device are both at `1.00`, and the surrounding context band is at `0.55`.

Canonical band values (blueprint §11.3, checklist `2.4`): `1.00` subject · `0.55` context · `0.60` heading · `0.30` background · `0.20` decision · `0.40` label.

### 3.2 ⛔ `motion_path` / modifier coherence rule
